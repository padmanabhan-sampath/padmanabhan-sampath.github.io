My doctoral research at the [Rolls-Royce@NTU Corporate Lab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rolls-royce-ntu-corporate-laboratory/about/){:target="_blank"} focused on condition monitoring of brushless synchronous generators (BLSGs) using wireless sensors. Rotor-side faults are among the most critical failure modes in BLSGs, yet internal rotor signals had been largely inaccessible to conventional monitoring systems. I developed a Wireless Condition Monitoring System (Wi-CMS) — establishing reliable RF links inside rotating electrical machines and demonstrating measurable improvement in fault diagnostic performance using rotor-internal signals.
