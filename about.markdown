Hi, I'm Padmanabhan Sampath Kumar – welcome to my blog!

I'm a Sensing Systems Engineer at Dyson, developing sensing technologies for new product categories. 

I've worked across diverse domains spanning robotics, machine health monitoring, wireless communication, indoor sensing, and industrial automation; all centered around developing sensing systems that interpret the physical world to drive intelligent action.

My interests lie at the intersection of sensing, machine intelligence, and embedded systems. This blog is where I share my personal projects from amateur radio and embedded design to photography.
